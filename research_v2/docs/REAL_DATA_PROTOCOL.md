# A-share follow-up protocol — not executed in this round

Entry requires repeated synthetic support in at least two independent mechanism/generalization settings, stable scenario approximation, and an explanation of gains relative to model averaging and empirical Bayes where applicable. This document does not authorize or report a new backtest.

Audit native histories before artificial masking: listing/delisting dates, total-return/adjustment definitions, suspended securities, price limits and tradability, corporate actions, duplicate dates, universe membership known at the decision date, and survivorship. Native short-history and artificially masked results must be separate.

Use chronological training/validation/test segments with lagged execution, fixed rebalancing, a common feasible universe, consistent treatment of non-tradable positions, and shared turnover/cost assumptions. Tune the same core methods on pre-test data only; no selection of years or tail parameters after observing test rankings.

Population covariance regret is unobservable in real data. Primary outcomes are realized future risk, turnover and performance under a common cost model. Any ex-post oracle is labeled a hindsight diagnostic and is not an executable benchmark. Informative missingness, serial dependence and regime shifts require empirical stress labels rather than inherited Gaussian guarantees.
